# Figure Captions

## `main_result.png`

StressLab's flagship summary figure. Across five topology families, collapse risk accelerates inside a compact utilization band, while follow-up evidence shows that coupling mainly trims failure margin rather than moving the cliff itself.

## `collapse_overlay.png`

Cross-domain collapse probability curves over baseline utilization. Different network families fail at different exact points, but they still concentrate inside the same broad transition band.

## `phase_transition.png`

Per-topology transition curves with estimated critical points. The figure makes the shared regime visible without hiding the meaningful family-level differences.

## `fragility_heatmap.png`

Collapse probability heatmap over utilization and coupling. Utilization provides the dominant gradient; coupling sharpens risk once systems approach the transition.

## `early_warning.png`

Shared early-warning signals across fragile versus stable systems. Variance growth and recovery lag separate collapse-prone systems before failure becomes obvious.

## `feature_laws.png`

Top collapse predictors and best interpretable law candidates from the flagship study. Utilization dominates, while structural features such as centralization and routing entropy refine the picture.

## `healthcare_case.png`

Concrete healthcare narrative: baseline behavior, collapse under stress, and improved behavior after intervention. This figure makes the synthetic cross-domain result legible in an intuitive operational setting.

## `controlled_thresholds.png`

Controlled follow-up collapse curves across coupling regimes. The threshold remains utilization-led even when coupling is deliberately varied.

## `threshold_shift.png`

Estimated threshold movement between low- and high-coupling regimes. The shifts are real but modest, which weakens any claim that coupling alone sets the collapse point.

## `failure_margin.png`

Mean change in minimum shock-to-failure margin under higher coupling. This is the strongest follow-up evidence that coupling matters by reducing robustness, not by relocating the threshold much.

## `healthcare_validation.png`

Healthcare family validation under arrival-intensity sweeps. The same cliff pattern appears in a more intuitive real-world-style setting, with collapse turning on near a 0.98x arrival multiplier.

## `followup_main_result.png`

Controlled follow-up summary figure. It condenses the full refinement of the claim into one view: utilization leads the threshold, coupling compresses the buffer to failure.
