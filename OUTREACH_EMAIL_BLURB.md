# Outreach Email Blurb

I wanted to share a project I have been building called StressLab. It started as a resilience-testing framework for queue, flow, and dependency networks, and has evolved into a discovery engine for systemic fragility. The core result from the first major studies is that collapse thresholds appear to be primarily utilization-led across several synthetic network families, while coupling mostly narrows the failure margin rather than shifting the threshold much.

The project now includes large-scale synthetic studies, theory analysis, publication-style writeups, and concrete case-study artifacts. If this intersects with your work in operations, infrastructure, markets, resilience, or complex systems, I would be glad to share the study package or walk through the strongest results live.
